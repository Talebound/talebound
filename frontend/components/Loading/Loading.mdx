# Loading

Renders a Loading component. Comes in different sizes

## Sizes

xs, sm, md, lg

<div className="sb-unstyled">
  <Loading size="xs" />
  <Loading size="sm" />
  <Loading size="md" />
  <Loading size="lg" />
</div>

## Colors

primary, secondary, tertiary, white, black, success, danger

<div className="sb-unstyled">
  <Loading color="primary" />
  <Loading color="secondary" />
  <Loading color="tertiary" />
  <Loading color="white" />
  <Loading color="black" />
  <Loading color="success" />
  <Loading color="danger" />
</div>

It is possible to also use "currentColor". Example of Loading component inside of `<Text color="info">`

<div className="sb-unstyled">
  <Text color="info" size="lg">
    <Loading color="currentColor" />
    <Text i>Loading...</Text>
  </Text>
</div>
